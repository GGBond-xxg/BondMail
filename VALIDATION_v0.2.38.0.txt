Recommended device regression

   normal and themed launcher icons match the supplied PNG files.
2. Pull to refresh while the app is visible and confirm new rows appear without a system alert.
3. Leave the app and receive genuinely new mail through background polling; verify the new
   notification channel can sound, vibrate and show a heads-up banner under system settings.
4. Open an uncached message and confirm the loading card fills the usable body area.
5. Open a short text/attachment message and confirm the card springs down before HTML reveal.
6. Open a long newsletter and confirm the sheet remains expanded and fades into scrollable HTML.
7. Expand/collapse more than three accounts in the drawer and confirm the rows and following
   destinations move continuously rather than appearing instantly.
